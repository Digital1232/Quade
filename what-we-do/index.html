What we do

of engineering services
Architectural Millwork: Where Craftsmanship Meets Precision
Forget cookie-cutter solutions. We collaborate with you to design custom millwork masterpieces that elevate your space, be it a cozy kitchen or a grand lobby. Our expertise in AutoCAD ensures flawless execution, and we provide:
  • Detailed Material Callouts and Specifications: Ensure accurate material selection and ordering.
  • Floor Plans and Elevations: Visualize the placement and dimensions of your millwork elements.
  • Section Drawings and Joinery Details: Understand the intricate connections and construction methods.
  • Fabrication-ready Drawings: Empower your contractors with clear instructions for accurate execution.
Laboratory Detailing: Safeguarding Innovation
From healthcare labs to cutting-edge research facilities, we understand the unique needs of every scientific environment. Our Kewaunee-compliant drawings ensure safety and efficiency, and we provide:
  • Comprehensive Casework and Fume Hood Design: Maximize functionality and meet safety regulations.
  • Epoxy Resin Surface and Worktop Specifications: Choose the right materials for your specific needs.
  • Adaptable System Integration: Ensure flexibility and future-proof your lab.
  • Accessory Specifications and Integration: Streamline workflow with essential lab equipment.
  • Fabrication-ready Drawings with Clear Instructions: Guarantee smooth installation and compliance.
Quantity Take-offs: Building with Confidence
We believe in the power of precision. Using Bluebeam software, we generate detailed and accurate quantity take-offs, ensuring your construction projects avoid costly surprises and stay on track. We provide:
  • Precise Material Quantities: Eliminate overruns and optimize material procurement.
  • Detailed reports with itemized breakdowns and cost estimations
  • Subcontractor bid packages for efficient project budgeting and scheduling
  • Real-time updates and revisions as project plans evolve
  • Data integration with your preferred construction management software (if applicable)
Custom Millwork Design: Beyond the Blueprint
Microvellum is our canvas, your imagination, the brush. We bring your custom millwork visions to life, from intricate cabinets to bespoke furniture, with meticulous detail and flawless cut lists. We provide:
  • Custom 2D and 3D millwork designs using Microvellum software
  • Accurate cut lists and detailed assembly instructions for fabrication
  • Material callouts with finish specifications and vendor recommendations
  • Prototyping and mockups for design validation and refinement
  • Expert Project Management: Seamlessly navigate the design and construction process
  • 3D Renderings and Virtual Walk-throughs: Visualize your final space before construction begins
