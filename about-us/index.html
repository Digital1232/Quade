About Us

Your Partner in Engineering Excellence

QUADE Engineering Services is a startup company founded by a team of engineers with over 15 years of combined experience, poured into shaping your Millwork spaces and laboratory dreams into flawless realities. From the meticulous millwork of AWI and NAAWS standards to the precision of Kewaunee labs and flawless quantity takeoffs, we’re your trusted partner in crafting built environments that inspire.

Our company believes that everyone deserves access to high-quality engineering services, regardless of their budget or project size. This is why we offer a diverse range of services at competitive prices. We leverage the latest technologies and best practices to deliver innovative solutions that meet the unique needs of each project. We are committed to building lasting relationships with our clients and helping them achieve their goals.
